# Contractor Onboarding Checklist

1. Signed independent contractor agreement and NDA.
2. Proof of certifications uploaded (scanned copies with expiry dates).
3. Background check (required for healthcare clients).
4. Payment details and W-9.
5. Site-specific induction scheduled by the project manager within the first 2 days.
6. Equipment: contractors supply their own hand tools; the company provides PPE.

Onboarding normally takes **3 business days**. Contractors cannot be assigned a start date earlier than 3 business days after onboarding begins.
