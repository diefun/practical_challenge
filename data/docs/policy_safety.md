# Site Safety Policy

- Every on-site contractor must hold at least **OSHA 10**. Client MSAs may require OSHA 30 (Northwind Health and Bluepeak Energy do).
- Each active construction project with more than 5 on-site workers must have a designated safety inspector (Safety Inspection level 3+).
- Incidents must be reported within 24 hours by raising a work order with priority **critical**.
- Stop-work authority: any contractor may stop work they believe is unsafe without penalty.
- Heat-illness prevention training is required for outdoor work in Phoenix between May and September.
