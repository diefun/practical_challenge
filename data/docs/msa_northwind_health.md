# Master Services Agreement — Northwind Health (CL01)

**Effective:** 2026-01-01 through 2028-12-31.

## Staffing terms
- All contractors working on Northwind Health premises must hold a current **OSHA 30** certification. OSHA 10 is not sufficient for healthcare sites.
- Contractors entering active clinical areas must complete Northwind's infection-control briefing (2 hours, billable) before their first shift.
- Northwind requires **10 business days** of notice before a contractor is removed from a project, except for safety violations.

## Rates
- Not-to-exceed rate for any individual contractor: **$125/hour**.
- Overtime above 40 hours/week is billed at 1.5x and requires written pre-approval from the Northwind project sponsor.

## Insurance
- General liability of $2M per occurrence. Background checks required for all staff working in patient-facing areas.
