# Rates, Overtime and Timesheets

- Standard week: 40 hours. Overtime is billed at **1.5x** and must be approved in advance by the project manager.
- Weekend work is billed at 1.5x; public holidays at **2x**.
- Timesheets are logged per day, per project, in 0.5-hour increments. Maximum 12 hours per day.
- Timesheets older than 14 days cannot be logged without finance approval.
- Rate increases take effect on 1 January each year and are capped at 5%.
