# SOW — Helios Solar Farm (P002), Bluepeak Energy

**Scope:** 25 MW ground-mount solar array near Phoenix, including inverter pads and interconnection.
**Term:** 2026-12-01 to 2027-08-31. **Budget:** $3.4M.

## Staffing plan
- 3 solar installers (Solar Installation level 4+), NABCEP certification strongly preferred.
- 2 electricians (Electrical level 4+) for inverter and switchgear work.
- 1 project manager (Project Management level 4+), PMP preferred.

## Site conditions
- Summer work hours shift to 5am–1pm from May through August due to heat.
- Heat-illness prevention training is mandatory before starting.

## Status
- Project is in **planning**. Mobilization starts 2026-12-01 once the interconnection permit is issued.
