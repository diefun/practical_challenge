# Work Order Guidelines

Work orders track discrete tasks, defects and incidents on a project.

| Priority | Use for | Response target |
|---|---|---|
| low | Cosmetic issues, nice-to-haves | 10 business days |
| medium | Standard tasks and defects | 5 business days |
| high | Issues blocking a milestone | 2 business days |
| critical | Safety incidents, outages | Same day |

Every work order needs a project, a clear title, and a description of the expected outcome.
