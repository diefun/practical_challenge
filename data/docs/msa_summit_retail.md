# Master Services Agreement — Summit Retail Group (CL03)

## Staffing terms
- Store remodels run overnight (10pm–6am) to avoid disrupting trading hours. Night shift carries a **15% premium**.
- OSHA 10 is the minimum safety certification for Summit sites.
- Contractors must be local to the store's metro area; Summit does not reimburse travel.

## Rates
- General trades (carpentry, drywall, plumbing): not-to-exceed **$95/hour** before night premium.

## Payment
- Net-45 payment terms. Timesheets must be submitted weekly by Monday 12:00 local time.
