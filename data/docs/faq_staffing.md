# Staffing FAQ

**Can a contractor work on two projects at once?** Only if both are part-time and total hours stay under 40/week; full-time assignments cannot overlap.

**Who approves rate exceptions?** The account director, in writing.

**How do I extend an assignment?** Update the assignment end date in the assignment system before the current end date passes. Extensions beyond 6 months need a fresh certification check.

**What happens when a project goes on hold?** No new contractors are mobilized. Existing assignments continue for up to 10 business days for demobilization, then end.

**Can remote contractors be assigned on site?** No. Remote contractors are limited to design and coordination roles (BIM, scheduling, project controls).
