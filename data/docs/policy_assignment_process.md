# Contractor Assignment Policy

1. **Skill match:** A contractor may only be assigned to a role if their skill level meets the project's minimum level for that skill.
2. **Certifications:** Required certifications must be valid on the assignment start date *and* through the planned end date. Expired or expiring certs block assignment until renewed.
3. **Availability:** A contractor cannot start before their `available_from` date and cannot hold two overlapping full-time assignments.
4. **Rate caps:** The contractor's hourly rate must not exceed the client's not-to-exceed rate in the MSA. Exceptions need written approval from the account director.
5. **Location:** Prefer contractors based in the project's metro area. Remote contractors may only take design or coordination roles.
6. **Notice:** Removing a contractor requires the notice period set by the client MSA.
7. **Records:** Every assignment change must be recorded in the assignment system (never by email alone) so the staffing graph stays accurate.
