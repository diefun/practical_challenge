# SOW — Project Apollo (P001), Northwind Health

**Scope:** Fit-out of a 40,000 sq ft outpatient diagnostic center in Austin.
**Term:** 2026-09-01 to 2027-03-31. **Budget:** $1.2M.

## Deliverables
1. Electrical rough-in and finish for imaging suites (requires Electrical level 4+).
2. Medical gas and plumbing tie-ins (Plumbing level 3+).
3. Weekly safety inspections with written reports (Safety Inspection level 3+).

## Milestones
- Rough-in complete: 2026-12-15
- MRI suite shielding sign-off: 2027-01-30
- Substantial completion: 2027-03-15

## Special requirements
- MRI suite work requires contractors to pass a ferromagnetic-safety screening.
- Two electricians are needed on site from 2026-11-24 to keep the rough-in milestone on track.
