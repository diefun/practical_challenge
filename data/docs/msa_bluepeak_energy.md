# Master Services Agreement — Bluepeak Energy (CL02)

**Effective:** 2025-07-01, auto-renews annually.

## Staffing terms
- Electrical and solar workers must hold **OSHA 30** plus either a Master Electrician License or **NABCEP PV Installer** certification.
- Certifications must remain valid for the full planned assignment period; a certification expiring mid-assignment requires renewal proof before the start date.
- Remote contractors may be assigned to Bluepeak HQ Retrofit (P005) for design-only roles (e.g. BIM Modeling).

## Rates
- Electricians and solar installers: not-to-exceed **$115/hour**.
- Project managers: not-to-exceed **$120/hour**.
- Travel for sites more than 100 miles from the contractor's home base is reimbursed at cost.

## Termination
- Either party may end an individual assignment with **5 business days** notice.
